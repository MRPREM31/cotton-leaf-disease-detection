==========================================================================================
CROSS-MODEL ERROR COMPARISON (BACTERIAL BLIGHT, CURL VIRUS, HEALTHY LEAF)
==========================================================================================

1. Bacterial Blight Confusion:
   - All models experience significant OOD over-prediction on Bacterial Blight images (misclassifying 135 to 186 images into the 4 non-target classes, notably Leaf Redding and Leaf Variegation due to reddish-brown necrotic spots).
   - Swin-T achieved the highest Bacterial Blight Recall (60.27%, 270 TP), followed by ViT-B/16 (52.46%, 235 TP), YOLO26s (49.78%, 223 TP), and CottonNet V2 (43.08%, 193 TP).
   - Bacterial Blight is frequently confused with Curl Virus across CNN/backbones when leaf wrinkling accompanies bacterial lesions.

2. Curl Virus Confusion:
   - Swin-T achieved the highest Curl Virus Recall (91.13%, 380 TP), with ViT-B/16 close behind (84.41%, 352 TP) and CottonNet V2 (69.78%, 291 TP).
   - Healthy leaves are primarily misclassified as Curl Virus across all models (CottonNet V2: 45, Swin-T: 80, ViT-B/16: 58, YOLO26s: 98 images) due to natural leaf curling, venation patterns, and lighting glare in field photography.

3. Healthy Leaf Confusion:
   - ViT-B/16 achieved the highest Healthy Leaf Recall (79.29%, 337 TP), followed by CottonNet V2 (75.06%, 319 TP) and Swin-T (69.18%, 294 TP).
   - CottonNet V2 showed exceptionally high precision on Bacterial Blight (92.34%) and Healthy Leaf (86.22%).

==========================================================================================
CONFIDENCE & UNCERTAINTY ANALYSIS FOR INCORRECT PREDICTIONS
==========================================================================================

- Correct Predictions: High average confidence (>0.88 - 0.94) across CottonNet V2, Swin-T, and ViT-B/16.
- Cross-Target Confusions (e.g. Healthy Leaf predicted as Curl Virus): Moderate confidence (0.58 - 0.74), indicating perceptual ambiguity in leaf morphology.
- OOD Over-predictions (Target classes predicted as Leaf Redding / Variegation): High confidence (>0.75 - 0.85) when strong background soil textures or severe necrotic lesions mimic non-target training classes.
- YOLO26s-cls exhibited lower peak confidence on subtle textures, leading to diffuse probability distributions across classes.
